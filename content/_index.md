I'm Imene (pronounced _/iman/_), currently a PhD candidate (third and final year) at INSA Lyon within LIRIS lab and Esker. My work focuses on NLP and Agents for web navigation.

I worked as a Data Scientist for almost 2 years before starting my PhD. It was a great experience in industry, although I wanted to have a research experience for more challenge and the ability to create knowelgedge that would serve a potential community.

I enjoy NLP-related subjects and open source. I worked on the MTEB extension to French (embedding benchmark), and MMTEB, the scaling of MTEB to multiple languages, especially low-resource ones. Then I shifted my research agenda to work on web agents, i.e., agents that can navigate on web pages to solve a gievn task, like booking a trip or looking for information.

Aside from research, I enjoy hiking, reading books, and painting. I am also very curious, with a lot of energy and motivation to solve problems.
